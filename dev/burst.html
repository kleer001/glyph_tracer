Burst sandbox

A cleared cell throws its colour outward. Every particle travels along a ray from the cell it came from and nothing pulls it down — this board has no gravity, so a burst that falls would be the only thing on screen obeying a force the game does not have. Motion is polar: an angle that never changes, a radius that does.
