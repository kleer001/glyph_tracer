Glyph Tracer · scratch

Cell sandbox

All sixteen glyphs at once, each on a random ground with a different random ink, so every form is judged against a colour pair it did not choose. The sliders are the lengths in data/geometry.json and the highlights in data/gloss.json; the plate is drawn by the real drawTile() and drawGlyph(), so what you balance here is what ships. The palette itself is on the right — the swatches feed the same random draw.
