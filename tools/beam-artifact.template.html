The A shows its heading

A push fires along the direction its letter points, and the beam is the only thing that says which way the shove went. It leaves as a thin line, swells, and dissipates over about three cells — one envelope drives the width and the opacity together, so it reads as a single travelling thing rather than a line being faded out.

Reach is in cells, so a reach of 3 dissipates three cells out whatever size the board is
  drawn. chase pulls the beam's root along behind its tip: at zero it is a spear
  growing out of the cell, at full it is a bolt that travels and leaves nothing behind.
  swell sharpens the envelope's peak, which shortens how long the beam is visible
  without changing how long it takes to cross — and that one envelope sets the width and the
  opacity together, which is what makes it read as a travelling thing rather than a line being
  faded out. The beam is a straight round-capped stroke and nothing else. This page runs the
  game's own renderer over the shove the rules actually resolve.
