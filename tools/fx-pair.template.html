The swap you can follow, and the anchor that eats

Both were failing for reasons that had nothing to do with the beam. One was a scene that hid what happened; the other was drawing past the point where the rules had already stopped.

Why the beam stops

An anchor is already the end of a line as far as the rules are concerned. A shove that reaches one is swallowed there, and armEnd walks up to an anchor and no further. A beam sailing through it would be drawing past the point where the ability's effect actually ended — claiming a reach the piece never had.

It stops half a cell short, on the near edge rather than in the middle. A line ending dead centre would look like it went in, and it did not: the thing that goes in is whatever the anchor eats.
